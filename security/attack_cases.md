# VoiceShield AI - Detailed Attack Cases

## Case 1: CEO Voice-Cloning Fraud

- **Attacker:** Criminal using an AI-generated executive voice
- **Victim:** Employee or finance department
- **Voice:** High synthetic probability
- **Caller:** Unknown
- **Claim:** CEO
- **Request:** Transfer INR 5,00,000 immediately
- **Damage:** Financial loss and unauthorized transaction
- **Expected risk:** CRITICAL
- **Action:** BLOCK / ESCALATE
- **Verification:** Independent trusted communication channel

## Case 2: Bank Employee OTP Attack

- **Attacker:** Criminal using a synthetic voice
- **Victim:** Bank customer
- **Voice:** High synthetic probability
- **Caller:** Unknown
- **Claim:** Bank employee
- **Request:** OTP
- **Damage:** Account takeover or unauthorized transaction
- **Expected risk:** CRITICAL
- **Action:** BLOCK / ESCALATE
- **Verification:** Never share an OTP with callers

## Case 3: Government Official Impersonation

- **Attacker:** Criminal using a cloned official voice
- **Victim:** Citizen or employee
- **Caller:** Unknown
- **Claim:** Government official
- **Request:** Sensitive information or urgent action
- **Expected risk:** HIGH / CRITICAL
- **Action:** Secondary verification or block, depending on request

## Case 4: Family Emergency Fraud

- **Attacker:** Criminal using a cloned family-member voice
- **Victim:** Family member
- **Caller:** Unknown or unexpected
- **Claim:** Family member
- **Request:** Urgent money transfer
- **Expected risk:** HIGH / CRITICAL
- **Action:** Secondary verification through an independent channel

## Case 5: Employee Impersonation

- **Attacker:** Criminal using a cloned employee voice
- **Victim:** Another employee
- **Caller:** Unknown
- **Claim:** Colleague or employee
- **Request:** Confidential company information
- **Expected risk:** HIGH
- **Action:** Secondary verification

## Case 6: Synthetic Voice Financial Fraud

- **Attacker:** Criminal using an AI-generated voice
- **Victim:** Employee or customer
- **Caller:** Unknown
- **Claim:** Trusted person
- **Request:** Financial transaction
- **Urgency:** High
- **Expected risk:** CRITICAL
- **Action:** BLOCK / ESCALATE

## Summary

The most dangerous cases combine synthetic voice, an unknown caller, trusted-person impersonation, a sensitive request, high urgency, and a large transaction. Multiple high-risk factors require stronger action.
